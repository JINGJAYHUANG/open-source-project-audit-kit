# Examples

The committed audits are completely synthetic:

- `steady-library.audit.json` demonstrates broad evidence and passing gates;
- `promising-agent.audit.json` demonstrates a technically promising project capped by unresolved security, privilege, and context-fit evidence;
- `abandoned-plugin.audit.json` demonstrates failed license and privilege gates;
- `novel-workflow.audit.json` exercises the innovation-scouting profile and explicitly stops short of an adoption verdict.

They are designed to test scoring and reporting behavior. They are not reviews of real maintainers, organizations, repositories, or products.
